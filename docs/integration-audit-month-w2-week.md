### 워밍업

Start with a hinge-focused joint prep and then rehearse the first deadlift pattern with bracing quality.

1) Breathing + brace reset (2 minutes)
- 3–4 slow nasal breaths into the ribs, then a short “brace” practice: exhale halfway, lock ribs/abs, keep a neutral spine.

2) Joint prep (6–8 minutes)
- Ankles: 10 controlled knee-to-wall rocks per side.
- Hips: 10 bodyweight hip hinges to a box/bench height (stop when hamstrings take over).
- Thoracic: 6–8 open-book rotations per side.
- Shoulders: 10 band pull-aparts or scapular retractions + 10 arm circles (small to medium).

3) Posterior-chain activation (4–5 minutes)
- Glute bridge holds: 2 sets of 6 reps, pause 1 second at the top (stay braced, ribs down).
- Back-extension / hip-back extension prep: 8 controlled reps, hinge from hips (no lumbar collapse).

4) Hinge rehearsal (3–4 minutes)
- Empty-bar deadlift patterning: 2–3 sets of 3 reps at a slow tempo.
  Cue: bar stays close, hips and shoulders rise together, lats tight before the pull.

5) First-work set readiness (1 minute)
- 1–2 very light deadlift reps with the same setup and bracing you’ll use today—stop before any form drift.

You’re done when your brace feels consistent and the hinge stays smooth (no back rounding, no early hip pop).

5) Transition to the first working set
- Take 1–2 deep breaths, brace, and do a final scap set (no extra reps) so the first bench set starts crisp.

### 워밍업

Warm up for today’s light Olympic-technique focus (plus easy aerobic engine maintenance). Keep everything submaximal and crisp so your first hinge and first “support/landing” position feel stable.

1) Breathing + bracing reset (2 minutes)
- 3–4 slow nasal breaths into the ribs.
- 5 reps of “brace and hinge” (hands on hips, push hips back, ribs down, exhale through the effort).

2) Joint prep (4–6 minutes)
- Ankles: 10 controlled knee-to-wall rocks (each side), then 10 ankle circles.
- Hips: 8–10 bodyweight hip hinges to a comfortable depth.
- Thoracic: 6–8 reps of open-book rotations.
- Shoulders: 10 scapular push-ups + 10 band pull-aparts (or slow scap retractions if no band).

3) Hinge pattern priming (6–8 minutes)
- 2 rounds of: 6 slow hip hinges + 6 “bar-path” hip hinges (use a dowel/broomstick if available, keep it close to your body).
- Cue: hips back first, neutral spine, brace before you move.

4) Olympic-support stability primer (6–8 minutes)
- 2 rounds of: 5 tall-rack holds (elbows high, ribs down) + 5 controlled bodyweight landings into a stable receiving position (soft knees, stacked torso).
- If you have a bar: 5 empty-bar front-rack to overhead path rehearsals at slow tempo (no pressing to fatigue).

5) Transition to the first conditioning movement (last 2 minutes)
- 3–4 practice reps of the American kettlebell swing mechanics: hinge → snap to hip extension → let the bell float back.
- Stop each rep with your brace still intact; you should feel “ready,” not tired.
