### 워밍업

Warm up for Wednesday (posterior-chain + deadlift + box-jump AMRAP). Keep it short, joint-first, and focused on hinge/bracing quality.

1) Easy raise + breathing (2–3 min)
- Brisk walk/row/air bike at a conversational pace.
- 3–4 rounds of: inhale through the nose, long exhale to “set” the ribs and brace.

2) Joint prep (3–4 min)
- Ankles: 10 controlled knee-to-wall rocks per side.
- Hips: 10 bodyweight hip hinges (hands on hips, sit back, keep ribs down).
- Thoracic: 6–8 open-book rotations per side.
- Hamstrings/glutes: 8–10 leg swings front-to-back per side (small range, no bouncing).
- Shoulders: 10 scapular retractions + 10 arm circles.

3) Hinge activation (4–6 min)
- Glute bridge: 2 sets of 8–10 reps, pause 1 second at the top (exhale to lock ribs down).
- Hip hinge patterning: 2 sets of 5 reps with a dowel or hands on hips—same cues each rep: push hips back, neutral spine, bar path would stay close to the body.

4) Deadlift-specific ramp (4–6 min, no grinding)
- 2–3 progressively heavier practice reps of the deadlift pattern (from light to moderate), stopping each rep with perfect position.
- Focus points: lats tight, chest “proud,” brace before the pull, drive the floor away, keep the bar close.

5) Transition to the first movement of conditioning (box jump) (2–3 min)
- 2–3 low box step-ups or controlled box landings (stick the landing, quiet feet).
- 2–3 very easy box jumps (or jump-to-stick) to rehearse takeoff/landing mechanics—stop well before form degrades.

You should finish warm-up feeling ready to hinge and jump with low fatigue—no near-failure reps.

1) Breathing + bracing reset (1–2 min)
- 3–5 slow breaths into the ribs, then 2–3 short “brace and exhale late” reps (tight midline, ribs down).

2) Joint prep (3–4 min)
- Wrist rocks + wrist circles (both directions) for 30–45 sec.
- Shoulder circles (small to medium) 30–45 sec, then scapular push-ups (on hands or on rings) 6–10 reps.
- Hip opener flow: 6–8 controlled hip hinges to a neutral spine + 6–8 glute bridges.

3) Gymnastic position activation (4–6 min)
- Wall-supported handstand hold practice: 2–3 short holds (stop well before shaking/losing alignment).
- Hollow-body “shape” practice: 6–10 controlled reps (or 20–30 sec total) to lock in a tight midline for pressing.

4) Skill rehearsal for the first movement (6–8 min)
- Start with kipping_handstand_push_up technique at low effort: 3–5 very easy reps focusing on smooth kip timing, stacked shoulders, and a controlled finish.
- Then 2–3 more reps with slightly better rhythm (still leaving plenty in reserve).

Stop the warm-up once you can keep the same body line and shoulder position for every rep—no grinding, no failure—so the first working set of the day feels light and crisp.
