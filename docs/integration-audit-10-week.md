### 워밍업

Bench day warm-up (press + brace rhythm, low fatigue). Start with 2–3 minutes of easy movement (bike/rower or brisk walk) to raise temperature, then prep the joints and the first bench pattern:

1) Shoulder + thoracic prep (2–3 min)
- Scapular push-ups: 8–12 reps, slow and controlled.
- Band pull-aparts or light face-pulls: 10–15 reps, focusing on scapular retraction.
- Thoracic openers (hands-behind-head rotations on the floor): 6–8 reps/side.

2) Wrist/forearm + elbow tolerance (1–2 min)
- Wrist rocks (hands on wall or bench): 10–15 reps.
- Light elbow/forearm activation: 20–30 seconds of gentle pronation/supination.

3) Breathing + bracing (1–2 min)
- 3–4 rounds: inhale through the nose, expand ribs, then brace for 3–4 seconds (no movement), exhale slowly.
- Add 2–3 “bench setup reps” with an empty bar: lie down, set shoulder blades, plant feet, then practice the same brace timing.

4) Hip/hinge stability for a stable bench base (1–2 min)
- Glute bridge holds: 20–30 seconds x 2, then 6–8 reps.
- Dead-bug (slow): 6–8 reps/side, keeping ribs down.

5) First press pattern (the first movement of the day) (3–5 min)
- Empty-bar bench press: 2–3 sets of 5 reps with perfect setup (shoulders pinned, bar path straight, controlled touch and press). Stop each set with reps still in reserve—no grinding.
- Finish with 1–2 quick technique sets of 3 reps, same tempo, to lock in the press rhythm and brace consistency.
